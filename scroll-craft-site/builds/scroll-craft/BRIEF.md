# BRIEF · scroll-craft, a landing page for the skill itself

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** The request was a single instruction ("use this
Skill to create a landing page for this project, Apple-style, explaining this
project") with no interview offered, so the eight topics below are answered by the
builder from the project's own published material. Every factual claim on the page
is taken from the repository README, SKILL.md, or references/uniqueness.md as read
on the day of the build. Nothing is invented.

Product: `nateherkai/scroll-craft`, v0.3.0. An agent skill for building premium,
scroll-driven websites, with a real design standard. Ships as a Claude Code plugin
and as a folder any coding agent can read.

---

## Step 1 · The brief

**What it is, and who for.** An agent skill that tells a coding agent how to design
and verify a scroll-driven site, and refuses the shapes that make AI-made pages
recognisable. For developers and designers who use Claude Code, Codex or another
agent to build marketing pages, and who are tired of the output.

**What the visitor must believe by the end.** *The difference between a page that
looks generated and a page that looks designed is a standard, not a model.* One
sentence, and the whole page exists to install it.

**What the visitor does next.** Reads the skill. One action, one label:
**Read the skill**. It appears in the hero, in the install act, and in the close.

**What the visitor already has.** Nothing is required from them. This build uses no
supplied assets and generates none: the page is type, hairlines, and the page
measuring itself.

**Art direction.** Premium-minimal, cold instrument, one accent. Requested register:
Apple product page. Explicitly not the cream-and-brass craft palette, not the
violet-to-blue AI gradient, not a clay diorama. The subject is typography and
verification, so the imagery is typography and measurement: dimension lines,
brackets, real computed numbers.

### Vibe in three to five words
Cold instrument, keynote, exact, unhurried. References: Apple's product-page
typography (scale contrast doing all the work), a Swiss technical drawing
(dimension lines and callouts on a bare sheet), and a printed type specimen
(one family, shown at every size it has).

### The scroll journey, section by section
1. The failure everyone recognises, at poster scale.
2. What the failure actually is, named in two directions.
3. The turn: the skill plans feeling and structure in the same pass.
4. The peak: the page measures itself and shows the numbers.
5. The range: eight grammars, each one forbidding what the others require.
6. What it refuses, and what it approved (the 0.3.0 rebuild).
7. How to install it, with the real commands and the honest limitations.
8. The close, inverted to paper, quiet, one link.

### Energy curve
Calm and monumental, then a hard mid-page lift, then the loudest quiet moment on
the page (the measurement), then lateral speed, then small factual type, then
silence.

### The ONE moment
**The page measures itself.** In the peak act the page draws its own spec sheet
over its own type: a measure bracket under a paragraph carrying the real width in
`ch` and the real line count, a tracking callout on the display line carrying the
real computed em value, and a contrast plate carrying the ratio computed from the
resolved colours at the moment the act opens. Then the annotations wipe away and
the plain page is left. Nothing is asserted that the browser did not compute.

### One thing no site they have seen does
It grades itself in front of you, with numbers it read off its own layout, and then
it takes the grading away so you are looking at the thing it graded.

### How far from premium-minimal
Premium-minimal, at the far restrained end. The user asked for Apple-style, and the
subject is a design standard, so restraint is the argument rather than the costume.

### One unbroken world, or distinct scenes
Distinct scenes. Eight acts on a ground that drifts a few steps and then hard-cuts
to paper for the close. Not a continuous world: the subject is a set of separate
structures (eight grammars), and a flight would say the opposite of that.

### Assets they already have
None. No `KIE_AI_API_KEY` is set and none is needed: this build generates nothing
and spends nothing, which is a first-class route in the skill.

## Step 2 · Grammar, signature move, gate

### Grammar: typographic poster (uniqueness.md §2.5)
Type is the imagery. Scale contrast does every job a photograph would have done,
the wordmark is set as part of the composition rather than as a 14px bar item, and
there is no persistent nav at all.

**Why the other seven lost.**
- *Filmic one-shot*: needs a continuous camera move and a clip. The subject has no
  moving subject; a scrub would be decoration bought at a real asset cost.
- *Chaptered editorial*: would make the page a printed feature. The journey has a
  peak and a lateral act, not eight chapters of argument, and the close needs to
  invert rather than colophon.
- *Live surface*: the honest pitch of this product is close to "watch what it
  does", but that grammar bans display-scale type and app chrome would replace the
  poster. The user asked for an Apple-style page, which is type-led.
- *Continuous world*: requires worldflight and generated legs. Wrong subject, real
  spend, and the page's content is a set of separate grammars, not a place.
- *Gallery / catalog*: would label eight grammars like specimens, but a catalog
  forbids a single hero claim and this page has one.
- *Split stage*: there is no two-sided argument here. The page does not compare two
  things; it makes one case.
- *Rhythmic cutlist*: bans pinning, and the peak needs a held stage to be measured
  on. Speed would fight the register.

### Signature move: **the self-measure**
Coded in the page, driven off `--sc-p` and off measurements taken from the live
layout. Not a kit device, not a parameter change to one. Distinct from every move
in the author's illustrative twelve rows (velocity letter physics, split-flap board,
pigment brush, lamp cone, merging rails, thermochromic bloom): those animate
authored content, this one reads the browser's own computed values and draws them
as a technical annotation over the element it just measured.

### Fingerprint gate
The registry at `d:\traffic-status\scroll-craft-site\FINGERPRINTS.md` was empty, so
this is the workspace's first build and it has nothing to clear. Recorded anyway,
and checked against the author's twelve illustrative rows in `EXAMPLES.md`, which
do not gate this build: it shares the typographic-poster grammar and the
no-navigation treatment with `scrollcraft-showcase`, and it shares a small-type
inverted close family with that row and with `maison`. It differs from all twelve
on signature move, hero (a two-line sentence at composition scale, not one word at
24vw), and act shape (8 acts, ~14.2vh, two `reveal` acts and one `pan`, no bespoke
pinned hero).

## Step 3 · Feeling curve, written before the acts

| # | Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Same page. Every time. | recognition | Two lines at 13vw on a bare ground, nothing else moving |
| 2 | Two directions of failure | unease | Two full-bleed statements wiping in from opposite edges |
| 3 | One job, not two | clarity | A short kinetic headline and a lot of air, at the point where the page turns |
| 4 | It checks its own work | awe, then trust | The page draws dimension lines and real numbers over its own type, then removes them |
| 5 | Eight grammars | curiosity | Type travelling sideways, each grammar forbidding the last |
| 6 | What it refuses | resolve | A wiped band of the actual refuse list, then ten real names, small |
| 7 | Three ways in | competence | Real commands in mono, requirements as a spec sheet, limitations stated |
| 8 | Different every time | quiet | The ground inverts to paper, the smallest type on the page, one underlined link |

**The peak.** Act 4. The sentence a visitor would say to a friend: *"it drew
measurements over its own text, showed the real numbers, then wiped them off."*
It holds the largest span on the page (3.6vh against act 5's 2.2vh), it gets the
only authored annotation budget, and it is preceded by the quietest act on the
page.

**The tell-someone sentence.** It is the site where the page measures its own
typography in front of you and then takes the measurement away.

**Authored silence.** Two places, both deliberate:
1. The opening 8% of act 4, where the stage holds a single hairline and the word
   VERIFY before any type arrives. The peak needs something to arrive from.
2. Act 3 is quieter than everything around it: one headline, one paragraph, one
   hairline, no device.

## Step 4 · The score

| # | Beat | Device | Span | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognition | `pin` + `--sc-p` scale | 2.2 | The poster holds while it shrinks a step: the claim tightening, not moving |
| 2 | Tension | `flow` + `reveal` (left, then right wipes) | — | A wipe is a change of state, which is what naming the failure is |
| 3 | Turn | `flow` + `kinetic` (lines) + `in` | — | The only assembling headline on the page, at the one place the argument turns |
| 4 | Peak | `pin` + bespoke self-measure | 3.6 | The measurement has to happen while the stage holds still |
| 5 | Range | `pan` | 2.2 | Lateral travel reads as breadth: eight options, not a hierarchy |
| 6 | Refusal | `flow` + `reveal` band + `in` stagger | — | The refuse list arrives as a wiped band, then the names settle under it |
| 7 | Install | `flow` + `in` | — | Administrative content, compressed: small type, real commands |
| 8 | Close | `pin` + hold cue | 1.2 | The page stops and the last screen stays on screen |

Device families used: `pin`, `reveal`, `kinetic`, `flow/in`, `pan`, plus `drift` on
the ground. Six. No family twice in a row. No `scrub` anywhere: this page has no
video, by design. 8 acts, measured at **13.9 viewport-heights** by the Step 5
pass. The length lands beside the 13.6-13.8vh band the earlier builds occupied,
so the separating dimension is the act count and shape (8 acts, four of them
pinned or panning, no scrub), not the total scroll.
